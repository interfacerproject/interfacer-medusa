# Local storefront

P6b's pinned minimal, legible brief governs this Operate surface. A plain light work surface, dark green action controls, high-contrast amber local-dev banner, system body typography and monospace only for identifiers/JSON. Two named journeys, native buttons and disclosure details, visible order/milestone/receipt progression. No photography or invented product imagery. Responsive single-column flow; focus, busy, empty, error and sold-out states are explicit. Strategy is task completion, not marketing.
